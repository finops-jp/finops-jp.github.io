# Cloud Sustainability and Its Intersection with FinOps

**Summary:** FinOps and cloud sustainability share a common goal: improving the efficiency of technology usage to deliver both financial and environmental outcomes. FinOps Practitioners, engineering teams, and sustainability stakeholders can combining cost visibility with emerging emissions metrics and reporting frameworks so that their organizations can make more informed decisions about infrastructure usage and architecture. All personas should become familiar with key sustainability concepts, terminology, and practical considerations for integrating environmental accountability into FinOps practices.
