# U.S. Public Sector FinOps Playbook

**Summary:**

The U.S. Public Sector FinOps Playbook provides guidance for Federal agencies to manage, budget, forecast, and optimize their rapidly growing cloud spend by building upon the standard FinOps Framework. Learn how core FinOps principles apply within the unique constraints of government procurement and operations, focusing on minimizing the “attenuation” (friction) that occurs when acquiring and using cloud services. Practitioners must take action by proactively engaging in collaboration across engineering, finance, and acquisition teams to ensure cloud spending decisions are driven by data and aligned to maximum business value for the agency.

The Government Cloud Market was valued at 14.93 billion in 2019 and is expected to reach 41.86 billion by 2025. With this growing cloud spend, the need to manage, budget, forecast, and optimize grows larger. FinOps, or cloud financial management, enables organizations to get the maximum business value by helping engineering, finance, technology, and business teams collaborate on data-driven spending decisions.

[This U.S. Public Sector FinOps playbook](</wp-content/uploads/2022/10/FinOps-Foundation_US-Gov-Playbook.pdf>) seeks to build upon the existing FinOps framework to assist federal agencies with their cloud spend. In our combined years of experience within various government agencies and/or cloud, we have noticed the attenuation that can occur when procuring and using cloud.

![](https://www.finops.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/us-gov-playbook.png)

We recognize that in some areas, the plays and guidance provided within the playbook may be at a high-level. As such, we plan to continue to expound upon this playbook with “specialty plays.”
